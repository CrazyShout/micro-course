## 怎样答卡片 / Answering the cards

先遮住答案，说出核心结论，再解释理由。计算题写出关键中间步骤；中文用于理解，随后尝试用英文独立回答。若只记得句子却解释不清，回到对应例题重做；编程题则需要实际运行。

Hide the answer, recall the conclusion and explain why. Show key steps in calculations. Use Chinese to understand, then try an independent English answer. If you remember a sentence but cannot explain it, redo its worked example; programming tasks require running code.
